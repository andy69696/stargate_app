Method and data notes

Source: HubSpot deals with a Start Date and End Date whose delivery window overlaps the selected year (UBDS financial years run 1 December to 30 November). Statuses follow the HubSpot Forecast status property, from Won Closed down to Pipeline; Not Forecasted deals are excluded. Each status can be switched on or off above; Pipeline and Forecast Placeholder are the least reliable and include a number of round-number planning records.

Full deal value counts each deal's whole Amount in company currency once in every year it touches, so a multi-year contract appears in full in each of its years. Pro-rated to year spreads the amount evenly per day across the delivery window and counts only the days inside the selected year. Neither is the Kantata forecast.

The Managed Services filter works on Primary Service Offering: "Managed Services only" keeps just those deals, "Exclude" removes them. Deals with no offering recorded count as not Managed Services and are shown as Unclassified. Bid pipeline deals (the separate HubSpot bid pipeline, mostly framework PIN/EOI records) are off by default because their amounts are framework totals, not UBDS revenue.

Kantata SX figures come from the Salesforce sandbox ubds--fullsbx: engagement contract revenue, plus forecast revenue by month built from assignment period forecasts (P1 won, P2 won and committed, P3 everything forecast; each level includes the ones above it) and fixed-price period revenue, which carries the engagement's own probability. HubSpot deals are matched to Kantata engagements by the engagement code at the start of the name, so change notices roll into their parent engagement.

Account plan (manual) overlays the Tier 1 and Tier 2 account-plan opportunities maintained by the account teams outside HubSpot (accountplan.json, from the workbook FY27 Account Plan Opportunities Tier1 and Tier 2). Each opportunity carries a TCV (net revenue), a start date, a duration and the account team's own probability; in-year values are pro-rated by day over the duration, and "Weight plan by probability" multiplies by that probability. Opportunities that already have a HubSpot code are shown with it and may overlap a Pipeline record; the FY26 sheet is the prior year's plan and is kept for comparison. Rows re-budgeted to zero are excluded. The overlay never enters the Kantata comparison or the change history.

FY26 → FY27 growth ladder on each client card (FY27 tab only) is always pro-rated in-year and ignores the view toggles: FY26 actual is the account's Kantata FY26 net revenue from the production report P123 (P3: actuals to date plus the remaining forecast, as of 19 Oct 2026 13:27 GMT; HubSpot won pro-rated where the account has no Kantata line), then HubSpot FY27 P1 (won), P2 (+ Commit, Most Likely), P3 (+ Best Case, Forecast Placeholder), + Pipeline, + the FY27 account-plan opportunities at full TCV with the probability-weighted figure alongside. Each step shows its growth on FY26.

Client resolution: the DWP sub-entities are rolled into Department for Work and Pensions. Where a deal is associated with both a reseller and an end client (Softcat, Littlefish, KPMG, Alexander Mann, Secon, Oakwood, i-10) it is counted under the end client. Six change-notice deals had no company attached and were assigned by deal-name prefix; they are marked with an asterisk in the deal tables.
