Notes for Resource Management

Resource plan — what we believe it takes

The delivery assumption, kept separate from the basis on which we price (step 3): the roles, grades, days and skills we believe the outcome needs, which later become the Kantata resource assignments. Generic resource sets grade and cost rate from the Kantata rate table. Working days exclude weekends and UK bank holidays. Cost = cost rate × days. Skills (must/should, from Kantata capability types) and the SC flag are app additions on top of v10, captured for Kantata Required Capabilities and recruitment.

Pricing model — the basis on which we price

Separate from the resource plan: we may believe a SFIA 4 for 10 days delivers the work and still price a SFIA 5 for 12 days, or two people. Copy the plan down as a starting point, then change grade, days to use and bill rate as the client negotiation and costed risk require. Revenue = days to use × bill rate × FTE; engagement cost stays on the resource plan, so margin is priced revenue against what we believe it will take.
