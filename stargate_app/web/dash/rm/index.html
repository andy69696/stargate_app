Utilisation

UTILISATION

Purpose

A week-by-week view of every billable person over the next twelve weeks, showing who is booked beyond capacity and who has time unfilled.

Objective

Smooth the load on the over-utilised and find work for the under-utilised before the week arrives, reading the figures on the P1, P2 or P3 basis you choose.

Scope. Employees, suppliers and partners flagged as billable in Kantata (utilisation reporting not suppressed), excluding the Service Delivery and Client Success business units and anyone offboarded. Suppliers and partners are shown up to their contract end date and employees up to their leaving date; later weeks and months are greyed out and never counted as capacity.

Weekly availability. Capacity = 5 days per week (8 hours/day pattern) less UK bank holidays. Planned and unplanned absence, non-working days and shift rest days reduce capacity. Demand = forecast usage on client engagements at the chosen basis (P2, the default, is cumulative and already contains P1); non-billable allocations (internal, training, pre-sales, company time, UBDS-* engagements) are never demand and do not reduce bench capacity. Availability % = (capacity − absence − demand) ÷ capacity. Kantata splits weeks at month ends; those partial periods are merged back into Monday-start weeks. A week in which absence covers 80% or more of capacity is marked A and greyed.

Over utilisation. Included when availability is below 0% in any of the next 12 weeks (W1 is the current week; Prev Wk is shown for trend). Red below 0%, amber 0–10%, green above 10%.

Bench. Included when availability is 25% or more in any of the next 12 weeks. Green below 25% (fully utilised), amber 25–49%, red 50% and above (bench risk). Post-contract weeks are greyed and do not count.

Demand by capability. Forecast revenue by capability family and month from the Kantata forecast on the chosen basis: P1 is sold work, P2 adds Commit and Most Likely (cumulative), P3 is the full forecast including Best Case and Pipeline. Beneath each month is the unfilled demand: generic placeholder hours on the P3 forecast with no named person, shown in days (hours ÷ 8, the working day). A placeholder is work that is sold or forecast but not yet resourced; it is the Matcher's input. "Unmapped" holds people and placeholders whose Kantata business unit is not yet mapped to a capability family.

Basis. Every view can be read on three bases: P1 sold (contracted work only), P2 likely (P1 plus Commit and Most Likely, the basis the RM team agreed and the default) or P3 full forecast (P2 plus Best Case and Pipeline). The weekly views recompute availability from that level of demand; the margin view uses the revenue allocation and cost at that level; the exception counts in the tab headers follow the chosen basis.

Margin. Current month plus the next three. Revenue allocation = the P2 revenue Kantata's forecast ledger attributes to the person (fixed-price element revenue with no person is spread across the people booked on that engagement in that month by cost share); cost = P2 forecast cost on the person's assignments. Margin £ = revenue − cost; margin % = margin ÷ revenue; revenue £0 with cost above £0 shows −100%. Included when margin is below 25% in any live month. Red below 0%, amber 0–25%, no colour 25–40%, green above 40%. Average = total margin ÷ total revenue across the live months. Current project = engagements with P2 days this month; last assignment date = latest forecast end date across the person's client assignments.
