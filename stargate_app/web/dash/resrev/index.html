How the numbers are built

Source: Kantata's own forecast ledger (Performance Analysis, fact = Revenue), so each year's P3 / P2 / P1 total is the figure Kantata's forecast reports show, with Lost Closed excluded. T&M revenue is the ledger revenue Kantata holds against each person's assignments (actuals for closed months, forecast for open ones; for fixed-price work Kantata already spreads the element's revenue across the people booked on it). Fixed-price revenue is the remaining element-level revenue with no person behind it (milestones, annuities, product, expenses), allocated to the people assigned to that engagement in that year in proportion to their forecast cost.

Not yet resourced is the engagement-level forecast on engagements that have no delivery elements or assignments yet (proposals and "forecast at this level" placeholders synced from HubSpot, e.g. DEFR-CASC). It carries no SFIA level and sits on the engagement's business unit; untick it in Resource type to see only revenue that has people or generics against it. Generic placeholders (names starting #) are unfilled demand and are included. Resource Groups (e.g. IoC Team) are shared managed-service pools. SFIA level is the resource's grade in Kantata; Group is the Kantata business-unit group (UBDS, Rayo, 3B Data Security, UBDS India); capability group is the business unit.

Historical utilisation uses actual timesheeted days for employees only (suppliers, partners and generics excluded). Capacity = employee head-months in Kantata × UK working days; absence = planned/unplanned absence, non-working days and shift rest days; internal = training, pre-sales, company time and UBDS-* internal engagements; chargeable = days on client engagements. Utilisation = chargeable ÷ (capacity − absence). Values over 100% mean more days were timesheeted than there are working days (weekend or overtime work, or a part-month joiner counted as a full head). Only complete months are shown.
